• Does cost change by timeframe? No — the spread itself is the same number regardless of timeframe. What changes is how much it hurts: on a 1-hour chart with a ~40-point stop, the NAS100 spread is under 4% of the risk; on a 1-minute chart with a ~4-point stop, that same spread eats ~38% of the risk. So the rule is: a trade is refused outright if the round-trip cost is more than 15% of what the trade is risking — not a different spread number, a pass/fail filter per trade.
